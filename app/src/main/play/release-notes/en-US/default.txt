Fixed: "APDU error: 0x6d00" when importing from an NFC security key that requires a PIN.

• Typing the PIN while holding the key against the phone often caused the NFC connection to drop or reset, resulting in technical APDU errors.
• The flow has been updated: if your key requires a PIN, the app will ask you to enter it first, and then prompt you to tap the key again. The PIN is sent instantly on the second tap.
